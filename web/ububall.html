Temporary values; no DNA changes. Set each stat from 0 to 1023. Normal is 511. Applying values resets the field and score, keeping your planned shot.

How to play

Both teams launch at the same time. The CPU plans from the field positions, without seeing your arrows. All three ubus can strike the ball, block shots, bump opponents, and enter the goal nets. After motion stops, plan again from the new positions. A goal resets the field; first to five wins.

Stats are temporary test values, separate from DNA. Spin curves travel and affects collisions. Practice ubus are custom characters, not minted collectibles. Matches stay in this browser and award no mint items.
